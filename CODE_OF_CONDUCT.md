# Code of conduct

Treat people respectfully, use evidence in technical disagreements and welcome contributors with different experience. Harassment, discriminatory language, threats, doxxing and publication of private information are unacceptable in issues, pull requests and discussions.

Report conduct concerns privately to Prajwal Pratap Yadav at prajwalpratapyadav5@gmail.com. Include relevant links and context; avoid reposting sensitive information publicly. The maintainer may remove inappropriate content, restrict participation or ban repeated abuse. Reports will be handled with discretion; conflicts involving the maintainer should use GitHub's platform reporting channels.
