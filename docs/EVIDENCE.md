## Interpretation limits

See [DATA](DATA.md) and [insights](insights.md). Arithmetic tolerance is a review policy, not a measurement standard. Reported yield's derivation may differ from ratio of state-level production and area. Physical crop units, primary lineage, boundary treatment, overlapping categories and repeated rainfall/input fields constrain interpretation. No cross-crop productivity ranking, fertilizer effect, causal driver finding, official-government-data endorsement or report-refresh claim is made. Roadmap issues define the remaining manual confirmation work.
